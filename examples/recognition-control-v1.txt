We can usually see at least one thing that would make life kinder, fairer, or more whole. We may know what we would change, yet the path from knowing to doing is rarely simple. Part of us is ready to begin; another part can see what might be lost, disturbed, or asked of us along the way.

We care about the people who would feel the consequences. That care can make us patient and thoughtful, though sometimes it leaves us waiting for a certainty that never quite arrives. We are not without influence, but we cannot carry every change by ourselves. Some things depend on choices shared across a family, a workplace, a community, or a wider world.

For now, we can hold both truths: the wish for something different, and the limits around what we can make happen alone. A small opening may still matter.
