We want to stop accepting things simply because we have become used to them. There are things we carry quietly, things we keep putting off, and things that trouble us more than we usually let on.

We want change, but there is also something we are trying to protect. We want to make our own choices without having to face everything alone. Sometimes we know exactly what needs to happen. Sometimes we only know that this cannot be all there is.

We have made more effort than people always see. We can be patient and still be tired of waiting. What we need from one another is not necessarily another answer. It may be time, trust, or someone willing to take the next step with us.

We do not all need the same thing. But we want what matters to us to have a place in what happens next.
